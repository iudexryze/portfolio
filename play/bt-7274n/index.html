BT-7274 // COMBAT BRIEFING
How To Play
You are Pilot Jack Cooper, bonded to Vanguard Titan BT-7274. Fight through infinite waves of IMC forces — the deeper you go, the harder it gets.
Each turn: type a combat command → see the result → enemy responds. Manage your hull, shield, battery and core charge to survive.
The Battery Reroute sidebar minigame gives ±battery each round — complete it for an edge in combat.
Attack Commands
fire [#]Shoot enemy by number. Targets lowest HP if no # given. Costs 8 battery.
volleySuppressive burst — hits ALL enemies for moderate damage. Costs 25 battery.
rodeo [#]Rip enemy battery — massive damage on Titans & Reapers. 35% fail chance.
coreBT Arc Wave — devastating AoE. Requires 80%+ core charge.
Defense Commands
defendParticle wall — 60% damage reduction + 12 shield this turn. Free.
stealthOptical cloak — enemy attacks miss entirely. 20 battery, 4-turn cooldown.
grappleReposition — 65% chance to evade all incoming hits. 8 battery, 2-turn cooldown.
ejectEmergency eject — skips enemy attack but drops 35 shield.
Support Commands
stim+15 attack bonus & hull regen for 2 turns. 15 battery, 3 charges total.
repairSpend 30 battery to restore 20 hull.
deployCall BT support drop — +25 hull, +25 shield. Once per wave.
chargeSkip attack: gain +20 battery, +15 core. Enemy still acts.

Resources
HullYour HP. Reaches 0 → mission failed. Regens with stim only.
ShieldAbsorbs damage before hull. Regens 6–12 per turn automatically.
BatteryPowers abilities. Regens 5/turn. Reroute minigame gives ±10–20.
CoreBuilds 8–12/turn. Kills add 10 core; boss kill adds 40. Spend 80+ on Arc Wave.
Pro Tips
stealth is your panic button — use it when hull is critical.
Every kill reduces stealth & grapple cooldown by 1 turn. Every 3 kills recharges a stim.
Boss kills fully recharge ALL pilot systems — fight aggressively on boss waves.
rodeo on titans deals 50–90 dmg — faster than chip damage with fire.
Save core for reinforce waves or titan clusters — it clears whole squads.
Between waves: hull/shield restore. Spend battery freely at end of each wave.
